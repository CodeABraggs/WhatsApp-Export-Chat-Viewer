How do I export a chat from WhatsApp?

Android

  1. Open the chat and tap ⋮ (top right)
  2. Tap More → Export chat
  3. Choose Include media for photos and voice notes
  4. Save the .zip, then open it here

iPhone

  1. Open the chat and tap the contact or group name
  2. Scroll down and tap Export Chat
  3. Choose Attach Media
  4. Save to Files, then open the .zip here

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to open my WhatsApp chat here?

Yes. The file is read by your own browser and is never uploaded to any server. There is no account and no chat data leaves your device. You can even use the viewer offline.

How do I view an exported WhatsApp chat with photos and voice notes?

Export the chat with Include media (Android) or Attach Media (iPhone), then open the resulting .zip here. Photos, videos, voice notes, stickers and documents appear inside the conversation, just like in WhatsApp.

Can I open a WhatsApp .txt export?

Yes. Open the .txt file on its own to read the messages, or open the extracted folder to include its media files too.

Does it work with both Android and iPhone exports?

Yes. Exports from Android and iPhone are supported in many languages, with 12- or 24-hour clocks and any date format.

Can I search an old WhatsApp chat or jump to a date?

Yes. Use search to find any message, jump to a specific date, or drag the scroll bar to move through years of history in seconds, even in chats with more than 100,000 messages.

Is this an official WhatsApp product?

No. It’s a free, independent, open-source tool for reading your own chat exports. It is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.
